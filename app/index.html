Platinum roadmap — four stages

One playthrough is enough: the story runs five chapters, free-roam continues after the credits, and there is no mission replay — so the roadmap front-loads the one step that cannot be revisited (the Chapter 1 meditation, flagged below) and leaves everything else for calm cleanup. Sources & method
